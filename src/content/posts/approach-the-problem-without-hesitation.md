Today I am not going to talk about my side hustle but a thing happened at my office.

I work at a head office of an organization. There are 30 branches, all of which use an offline finance software. Every year after year end our head office collect the software from local branches. The way is Anydesk software and I being the incharge of the collection of the software. 

In last 1 and half month I have collected about 12 software out of 30. And I procrastinated a lot to ask local branch representatives for that. But my boss needs the software badly before next week. So today I started to ask the remaining branches that we need it. Then out of my surprise within 3-4 hours I was able to collect 6 software from 6 branches. It feels like unbelievable. Only a simple action of asking on whatsapp worked like a magic.

So I think today has a great lesson for me. And it is that -- I have to start without fear. I have to approach a problem without hesitation. Sometimes it is the limiting beliefs within us that hold us back. We just need to push ourself a bit to overcome that.

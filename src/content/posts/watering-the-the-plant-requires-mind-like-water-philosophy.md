For a week I have been struggling to complete the installation of Ubuntu Server on my laptop. As I wrote about it in my first post there was a mismatch between Vbox kernel and my linux kernel. I fixed it and the installer ran. But I postponed the installation that day as it took a while to fix the issue. My plan was to do it next time I sit. But I couldn't sit another time in a week. There are family responsibilities for which I can't usually open my laptop at home. Yesterday I did when my son fall asleep but my son woke up within 30 minutes and I had to suspend my work again. I put the laptop on a sleep mode in charging for whole night so that I can start from there once I wake up. But I couldn't wake up early today. I wake up at 10am and I had to prepare myself for office so I did just that. 

You see this is how my life is operating at this moment. But I want to make it an end.

As you see I have family and job responsibilities but that doesn't necessarily mean I don't have time. I think it's all about clarity. Yesterday a very thing happened first time in my last 3 years in office. I am incharge of paying utility bills at my office. And internet bills have due dates before 14 of the month. And I never forgot to pay before due date only until yesterday. Someone from my office asked whether I did pay the bill at the afternoon and I was shocked as I didn't. 

I passed the whole Thursday setting up this blog from my office. And only did I think not more than 30 minutes of office work. But I forget to pay the bills on Thursday which is a shame.

I was focused on something else. 

But now I think I should enforce a limit on how much time I can focus on recreation at my office. It should be in control. My mind should be a mind like water. Not stressed, not overwhelmed, not distracted, with full intention.

Fortunately I have my Daily Leverage Method, which can help in that. 

I just have to use it with discipline.
